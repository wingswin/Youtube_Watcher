- **Kubernetes 1.35 deep dive (60 enhancements):**
  - **Gang Scheduling (alpha, disabled by default):** New workload API ensuring all pods in a group are scheduled or none — solves GPU deadlock where partial scheduling burns resources with no progress. Six years from alpha to GA.
  - **AI/ML focus:** Distributed training jobs no longer lock up cluster resources with partially-scheduled pods.
  - Applicable beyond GPU — any resource contention scenario (storage, networking).
