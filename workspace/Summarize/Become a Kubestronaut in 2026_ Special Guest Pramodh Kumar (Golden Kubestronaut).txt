### Part 2: Channel Context

**Relation to Broader LLM Themes:**  
This content focuses on **applied AI/ML infrastructure** rather than theoretical LLM research. Key themes include:
- **Agentic Workflows**: Google ADK course for building AI agents, aligning with the trend of autonomous LLM-driven systems.
- **RAG & Fine-tuning**: Not directly covered, but the AWS AI Practitioner cohort introduces foundational AI/ML concepts (e.g., LLMs, parameters) that underpin these techniques.
- **Cohort-based Learning**: Emphasizes hands-on, community-driven skill-building for deploying AI workloads (e.g., hosting AI agents on Kubernetes).

**Channel Perspective vs. Other AI Channels:**  
- **Developer-focused**: KodeKloud targets DevOps/cloud engineers, contrasting with academic channels (e.g., Stanford CS224n) that focus on LLM theory.  
- **Practical vs. Theoretical**: Prioritizes certification prep and real-world deployment (e.g., Kubernetes for AI agents) over model architecture or training.  
- **Community-driven**: Uses cohorts and leaderboards to motivate learning, unlike channels like "Two Minute Papers" that highlight research breakthroughs.  
- **Multi-cloud**: Covers AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud AI services, appealing to practitioners needing cross-platform skills.
