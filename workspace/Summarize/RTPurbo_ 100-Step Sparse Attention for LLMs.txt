### Part 2: Channel Context

**Relation to Broader LLM Themes:**  
This content addresses the critical challenge of scaling LLMs to long contexts (e.g., 1M tokens) by optimizing attention mechanisms—a core bottleneck in transformer architectures. It highlights the trend toward *post-training efficiency* (sparsification without full retraining), contrasting with approaches that require expensive native sparse pre-training. The paper's focus on maintaining accuracy while reducing compute aligns with industry priorities for deploying LLMs in real-world applications like document analysis and code generation.
