### Part 2: Channel Context

**Relation to Broader LLM Themes:**  
This video provides a historical and conceptual overview of the shift from rigid, workflow-based automation to agentic LLM systems. It emphasizes the practical evolution of agent design (prompt → context → harness) and ties it to concrete examples like Cursor, Lovable, and Perplexity. The content aligns with the broader industry trend of moving beyond simple RAG or fine-tuning toward autonomous, tool-using agents.

**Channel Perspective vs. Other AI Channels:**  
KodeKloud is a **developer-focused, hands-on learning platform** (primarily known for DevOps and cloud training). Compared to academic channels (e.g., Yannic Kilcher, Stanford MLSys) that emphasize theory and research papers, KodeKloud’s perspective is more **pragmatic and applied**—it explains concepts through real-world use cases and historical progression rather than mathematical depth.
